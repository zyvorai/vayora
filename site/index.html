₹

Rupees, exactly

Every amount is an integer in paise. Never a float, so ₹4,99,000.00 is always ₹4,99,000.00.

UPI

Scan, tap, pay

The checkout shows a QR or opens the customer's own UPI app. No UPI ID to type. A QR is never proof of payment: only a verified result is.

GST

Invoices that file

CGST, SGST and IGST by SAC code, GSTIN-checked, with a GSTR-1 style summary. Zyvor does not file returns for you.

Principles

Six promises the code keeps.

Never charges twice.

Every write carries an idempotency key. When a provider does not answer, the payment becomes unknown, and nothing new can start, through any provider, until it is resolved. Zyvor asks the provider. It never re-sends.

  1. 1No answer from the provider
  2. 2Marked unknown. New attempts blocked.
  3. 3Zyvor asks the provider
  4. 4Confirmed: captured, once

Explains every rupee.

Open a settlement and see the payments, refunds, fees and adjustments behind its net, and the bank line it matched. In acquire mode, settlement batches and escrow proof show what sits in sponsor custody before payout. Anything that does not add up becomes a case someone can own.

Books you can audit.

An append-only, double-entry ledger, balanced per currency by the code and again by a database constraint. Orchestrate posts provider receivables; acquire posts escrow and merchant payable. Nothing is edited. A correction is a new entry that reverses the old one.

Routes with receipts.

A capability matrix and your rules choose where a payment goes, and the decision records every provider it considered and why it picked one. No black box.

Example routing decision · UPI, no UPI ID

  • CashfreeIssues a UPI intent and QR (opt-in per account). Chosen.
  • PhonePe / sponsor_upiAvailable when connected for orchestrate or acquire.
  • RazorpayNo UPI intent capability. Skipped.

Runs the shop floor.

Branches and counters, each with its own QR. Cash entries, an end-of-day closing a second person signs off, and a plain-words timeline for every payment. Till cash is records only. Digital money moves through your provider (orchestrate) or the sponsor rail (acquire).

  2. 2Cash entry
  3. 3Signed by a second person

Hard to abuse.

Tenants are isolated by row-level security, tested as a non-superuser. Webhooks are guarded against SSRF and replays. The audit chain is tamper-evident. The threat model says what is defended and what is not.

  • Row-level security
  • Rate limits
  • Expiring API keys
  • SSRF-guarded webhooks
  • TOTP and roles
  • Tamper-evident audit
  • Rotatable master key
  • Supply-chain scans

    Questions, answered straight.

    Is Zyvor a licensed payment service?

    No. In orchestrate mode your own provider collects and settles; Zyvor does not hold funds. In acquire mode a licensed sponsor PA / bank PSP holds escrow; Zyvor is the merchant-facing software and ledger. Live acquire needs counsel, a sponsor LOI, acquire KYC and an explicit env gate. See the regulatory position and acquire mode (not legal advice).

    What is acquire mode?

    A second money mode: custody ledger accounts (escrow:*, merchant_payable:*), settlement batches with a T+N delay, escrow proof, and payouts via a sponsor rail (send_rail). PhonePe and sponsor_upi adapters are in the tree at simulator level. Operator guide: ACQUIRE_MODE.md.

    What has actually been verified?

    The platform is tested against local simulators and a k3s sandbox. One capability, Cashfree UPI intent and QR, also ran against Cashfree's own sandbox. PhonePe, sponsor UPI, settle rails and card tokens are simulator/mock only. Nothing has run against a live provider for acquire. STATUS.md lists every gap.

    Can I use my own gateway or bank API?

    Yes. Built-in adapters cover Razorpay, Cashfree, PhonePe and sponsor_upi. A generic custom adapter speaks a small JSON contract. Routing, the ledger and reconciliation stay the same. See bring your own provider.

    Do the iPhone apps move real money?

    No. Vayora Pay and Vayora Merchant are test apps against the simulator. A real consumer UPI app needs NPCI TPAP approval and a sponsor bank, which these do not have.

    Can I start over in the sandbox?

    Yes. A test environment can clear its own payments and money records from Settings, with a typed confirmation. Your setup stays. A live environment cannot do this, and the reset is written to the audit log.

    Does it file my GST returns?

    No. It issues GST tax invoices and a GSTR-1 style summary. Filing is yours.

    What does it cost?

    The software is free under Apache-2.0. Your provider, bank and any licensed partner charge their own fees; Zyvor can measure the real cost from your settlements.
